Built around short decisions.

Fresh market sessions

Price paths reset whenever you sign in, preventing endless runaway simulated compounding while preserving account value between sessions.

Two-minute focus

KiwiEx defaults to a two-minute live window with five-second candles, plus 5m, 15m, 30m and 1h views.

Seed-generated collectibles

New NFT listings appear each session. Enter any seed to recreate an item; its compact seed and ownership travel with your KX5 key.

One-time NFT buyer fee

Direct purchases and accepted offers include a one-time 2.5% buyer fee, shown with the total before you buy. Listed sellers receive 97.5% of their sale price.

Portable KiwiEx accounts

KX5 keys carry cash, market positions and NFT seed data. Ledgered remains the bridge for cash entering or leaving KiwiEx.

KiwiEx is a fictional, simulated market project. It does not execute real cryptocurrency transactions.
