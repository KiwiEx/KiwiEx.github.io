About the project

A market designed to be watched.

KiwiEx is a fictional meme and alt-token trading environment in the Ledgered universe. It is intentionally separate from Exchangr. The market is built for rapid chart reading: short candles, volume, momentum, EMA structure, RSI and quick buy/sell decisions.

How sessions work

Every sign-in creates a fresh market session.The simulated price path is regenerated around each token’s current reference price. A session cannot compound upward forever; prices are pulled toward a session anchor and remain inside a bounded long-run range.
Short-term trades still matter.Breakouts, trends, consolidations, second legs, distributions and reversals still generate strong short-window movements. The default chart shows just two minutes.
Holding across sessions still works—just more slowly.Your KX3 key stores the marked value and cost basis of each open position. On a later sign-in, a small deterministic long-term carry is applied before that value is rebased onto the fresh session price. Carry is capped so waiting is not the dominant strategy.
Ledgered handles cash movement.Deposits deduct from a selected Ledgered account and add KiwiEx cash. Sales remain as KiwiEx cash until you deliberately withdraw them back into Ledgered.

Account keys

KX3 keys stay compact because they do not contain charts, trade ticks or market history. They contain account identity, KiwiEx cash and a compact marked-value snapshot of open positions. Transaction history remains local to the browser.

Simulation notice

KiwiEx does not connect to a real exchange or execute real cryptocurrency trades. Coin names may be based on recognizable meme-token markets, while the short-term trading behavior inside KiwiEx is simulated for the project.
